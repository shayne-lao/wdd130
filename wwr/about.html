History

The Evalia Whiterwater Rafting Company was founded in 1960 by a millionaire, named Mr. Poppyseed, and his wife, Mrs. Poppyseed who wanted to create a fun and exciting outdoor destination. It started as a small riverside attraction with only a few rafts and basic facilities. Over time, it became popular for its thrilling rapids and beautiful scenery.

As more visitors arrived, the resort expanded its facilities and added more activities for guests. It also began promoting environmental awareness and river protection. Today, the resort is a favorite destination for adventure, relaxation, and unforgettable memories.
